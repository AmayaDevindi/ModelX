# 10 – Glossary

**Finance**
- **ASPI** – All Share Price Index: the index of every share on the CSE.
- **S&P SL20** – index of 20 large, liquid CSE companies. The only stocks we cover.
- **CSE** – Colombo Stock Exchange. **CDS** – Central Depository System (where share accounts are held).
- **CBSL** – Central Bank of Sri Lanka. Publishes policy rates, T-bill/T-bond yields, FX rates, weekly indicators.
- **CCPI** – Colombo Consumer Price Index; the main inflation measure.
- **FD** – fixed deposit. **Real return** – return after inflation (an FD at 8% with 10% inflation loses value).
- **T-bill / T-bond** – government short-term (up to 1 year) / long-term borrowing. Low risk.
- **Unit trust** – a pooled fund run by a manager; types include money market, income, balanced, equity. **NAV** – net asset value per unit (the unit's price).
- **Debenture** – a company's listed bond.
- **P/E** – price ÷ earnings per share. **P/B** – price ÷ net assets per share. **ROE** – profit ÷ shareholders' equity. **Debt-to-equity** – borrowings ÷ equity. **Dividend yield** – dividend per share ÷ price.
- **Volatility** – how much returns jump around (standard deviation). **Correlation** – whether two assets move together.

**ML / modelling**
- **Baseline** – the simplest sensible prediction (here: the historical average). A model only "wins" if it beats this.
- **ARIMA / Prophet** – classic time-series forecasting models.
- **Walk-forward validation** – train on the past, test on the next period, roll forward. Never shuffle time.
- **MAE / RMSE** – average size of forecast errors.
- **Mean-variance optimisation (Markowitz)** – picks the split with the best return for a given level of risk.
- **Monte Carlo** – simulate thousands of possible futures to get a range and a chance of loss.
- **Calibration** – when we say "20% chance of loss", about 20% of such cases should actually lose.
- **FinBERT** – a free language model trained to score financial text as positive/negative/neutral.
- **Entity linking** – working out which company or instrument a piece of text is about.
- **OCR** – reading text from an image (for scanned PDFs).
